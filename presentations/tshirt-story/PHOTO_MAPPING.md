# Photo mapping

Your originals are in `source/photos/`, byte-identical copies that were never edited. The processed copies are in `images/`. Each copy is cropped to its frame, centred on the subject, and given one identical warm tone: gain R×1.04 G×1.00 B×0.91, plus a 6% sun-orange (#F2A65A) tint. The satellite images get no tone.
