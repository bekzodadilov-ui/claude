## Intent

A short professional documentary about consumer psychology and brand strategy:
"Liquid Death — How Marketing Made Water Cool". Dark, cinematic, rebellious,
premium, editorial and critically analytical. Heavy-metal album covers,
underground culture, premium beverage commercials, modern documentary motion
graphics. It must feel like a documentary, "not a commercial for Liquid Death".
